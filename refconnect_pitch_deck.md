# RefConnect Investor Pitch Deck

## Cover

**RefConnect**

Clinical readiness to confirmed referral

Nigeria’s digital referral coordination platform

## Slide 1

### Referral pathways are too often disconnected

- Nigeria’s care pathway depends on coordination between primary, secondary and tertiary facilities; continuity is frequently weak.[1]
- In a 2025 study of 1,025 residents across eight urban slums, only **7.4%** reported receiving a referral; verbal referrals were the dominant handoff method.[1]
- When clinicians cannot confirm capability, availability and acceptance quickly, patients lose time and facilities lose confidence.

**Takeaway:** referral is not just a transfer; it is a high-stakes matching problem.

## Slide 2

### RefConnect matches the patient to the right-ready destination

- **Search by procedure:** clinicians begin with the required intervention, not a static hospital list.
- **Rank by readiness:** a procedure-specific score translates hospital capability into a clear best-fit sequence.
- **Prepare a structured handoff:** stored patient profiles support a faster, more consistent referral request.

**Mission:** coordinate reliable referrals for healthcare centres in Nigeria.

## Slide 3

### Two portals create one accountable referral workflow

- **Administrator Portal:** authorised hospital teams update capability evidence and review readiness inputs.
- **Doctor Portal:** referring clinicians search, compare and select a destination from a clear ranked pathway.
- **Referral handoff:** the workflow keeps the selected procedure, destination and patient profile aligned.

**Design principle:** local facilities own the data; RefConnect makes it usable at the moment of referral.

## Slide 4

### The MVP already makes complex referral data usable

- **50** specialty-grouped procedures are available for search and comparison.
- **22** weighted capability factors power procedure-specific readiness calculations.
- **5** active showcase hospitals demonstrate editable institutional data and ranked destination results.
- A **100-record** inactive directory illustrates the onboarding pathway without presenting unverified facilities as ready.

**Current stage:** deployed product MVP; clinical validation and live institution onboarding are the next milestones.

## Slide 5

### Why now: policy and practice are converging around data exchange

- Nigeria’s Digital in Health Initiative prioritises electronic records and data exchange for medical facilities.[2]
- Its public aspiration is system-wide transformation anchored in data and driven by technology.[2]
- A 2025 scoping review finds digital health can strengthen utilisation, data quality and community engagement, while flagging connectivity, training and implementation constraints.[3]

**RefConnect response:** start with a focused, low-friction referral workflow and build trust through institutional data stewardship.

## Slide 6

### A focused Kano wedge can expand into a national coordination layer

| Initial buyer | Immediate job to be done | Expansion path |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hospitals and referral centres | Confirm capability and receive structured referrals | Multi-site hospital groups |
| State health authorities | Map capacity and coordinate networks | State-wide referral infrastructure |
| Clinical networks | Standardise referral pathways | Specialty pathways and national interoperability |

**Commercial discipline:** validate willingness to pay and implementation requirements through pilots before publishing market-size or pricing claims.

### Revenue can align with the value of coordinated care

- **Institution subscription:** recurring access for referral coordination, readiness records and workflow controls.
- **Onboarding and integration:** implementation support, data mapping and verified capability setup.
- **Enterprise layer:** network analytics, governance and integrations for states, groups and payers.

**Pilot objective:** establish the price, adoption motion and support model that fit Nigerian care delivery.

## Slide 7

### Progress is product evidence—not revenue traction yet

- A live two-portal MVP demonstrates role-gated data stewardship, procedure discovery and referral preparation.
- The product applies the same readiness engine to all **50** configured procedures across the five showcase institutions.
- The next proof points are live hospital participation, clinician activation, completed referrals and faster time-to-destination confirmation.

**Investor truth:** metrics should be reported only after real pilot operations begin.

## Slide 8

### Verified operating data can create a durable coordination layer

- **Procedure-specific capability model:** readiness is calculated from the clinical requirements of the intervention, not generic facility labels.
- **Dual-sided workflow:** both the receiving facility and referring clinician gain value from current, structured information.
- **Data network effects:** every verified capability update and completed referral improves the usefulness of the network.

**Competitive positioning:** RefConnect complements hospital systems and referral directories by coordinating the decision and the handoff between facilities.

### Roadmap: turn product evidence into live network proof

- Establish a 5–10 hospital pilot cohort and co-validate capability profiles with clinical champions.
- Track completed referrals, acceptance signals and time-to-destination confirmation.
- Prioritise institution-ready security, governance and interoperability only after validated workflow adoption.

## Slide 9

### The ask: turn a validated MVP into a live referral network

- **Pilot partners:** 5–10 hospitals and clinical champions to validate readiness inputs, workflow fit and referral outcomes.
- **Capital:** pre-seed funding amount to be finalised with founders; directed to product hardening, implementation, security and pilot operations.
- **Milestones:** verified institution onboarding, referral acceptance tracking, interoperability roadmap and measurable coordination outcomes.

**Team placeholder:** add founder names, clinical credentials, technical leadership and relevant operating experience before external investor circulation.
