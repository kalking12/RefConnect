# Stacstart Hackathon Research — RefConnect

**Source reviewed:** [Stacstart Borderless Bytes Hackathon](https://stacstart.com/hackathon#tracks), accessed 13 September 2026.

## Key published requirements

Stacstart describes the event as the **Borderless Bytes Hackathon**, held virtually across four countries. Teams must contain **two to four people**; individual participation is not allowed. Eligibility also requires registration for the Stacstart Summit.

The published participation sequence is: join the Hackathon Hub, introduce yourself, publish a social post using **#BuildWithStacStart**, and form a two-to-four-person team. The page lists a submission deadline of **28 September at 11:59 PM** and a grand finale on **8 October**.

## Tracks and best RefConnect fit

| Track | Published description | RefConnect fit |
|---|---|---|
| Access & Inclusion | Tools widening access to jobs, finance, healthcare, or education across Nigeria, Kenya, Uganda, or Ghana. | **Primary recommendation.** RefConnect coordinates healthcare referrals and helps clinicians identify destination readiness. |
| Civic Tech & Public Good | Tools addressing a public infrastructure, governance, or community problem. | Credible secondary framing if the team emphasizes referral-system infrastructure and health-system coordination. |
| Open / Wildcard | Useful projects outside the focused tracks. | Not recommended because the healthcare link to Access & Inclusion is direct. |

## Submission checklist

The submission page specifies a working deployed prototype or accessible application, a public GitHub repository URL, a video demo, and project details including title, target audience, and technology stack.

## Judging criteria

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Technical execution | 35% |
| Problem fit | 25% |
| Demo / communication | 20% |
| Originality & innovation | 20% |

## RefConnect implications

RefConnect already has a deployed prototype, a Doctor Portal, a role-gated Administrator Portal, procedure-level hospital-readiness ranking, and a referral-handoff flow. The key application priorities are to confirm a team of two to four members, make a suitable repository public, record a concise end-to-end demo, and position the product as an **Access & Inclusion** solution that converts hospital capability data into a practical referral decision.
